Privacy Policy

KoseFlow Chrome Extension  ·  Last updated: May 1, 2025

1. Overview

KoseFlow ("we", "our", "the extension") is a Chrome extension that automates image and video generation on Google Flow (labs.google). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is handled.

2. Data We Collect

When you sign in with your Google account, we store the following information in our database (Firebase Firestore):

  • Email address — used solely to identify your account and pre-fill checkout forms.
  • Plan status — free or pro, determined by license activation.
  • Daily usage count — number of prompts processed today, reset each day. Used to enforce the daily generation limit.
  • Usage date — the date the usage counter was last reset.

We do not collect, store, or transmit your generated images, video files, prompts, or any content you produce through Google Flow.

3. Authentication

Sign-in is handled entirely through Google OAuth 2.0 via the Chrome Identity API. We never see or store your Google password. The OAuth token is used only to obtain your email address and authenticate your Firebase session.

4. Payments

Pro plan purchases are processed by LemonSqueezy (lemonsqueezy.com), a third-party payment provider. We do not collect, process, or store any credit card or payment information. When you activate a license key, the key is verified with LemonSqueezy's API and then discarded — only the resulting plan status (pro) is saved to your account.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase — authentication and database (Firestore). Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google OAuth 2.0 — sign-in only. Google Privacy Policy
  • LemonSqueezy — payment processing and license verification. LemonSqueezy Privacy Policy

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any other third parties.

6. Data Retention

Your account data (email, plan, usage) is retained as long as you have an active account. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Upon request, we will permanently delete your Firestore document within 7 days.

7. Local Storage

The extension uses Chrome's local extension storage to persist your UI settings (e.g., model selection, concurrent prompt count, filename prefix). This data never leaves your device and can be cleared by removing the extension.

8. Permissions

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • sidePanel — to display the extension's control panel.
  • tabs — to detect the active Google Flow tab and relay the auth token.
  • downloads — to save generated images and videos to your device.
  • identity — to sign in with your Google account via OAuth 2.0.

9. Children's Privacy

KoseFlow is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, please contact us at: ai.kose.lab@gmail.com
