Frequently Asked Questions

What is KoseFlow?

KoseFlow is a Chrome extension that automates bulk AI image and video generation on Google Flow (labs.google). It lets you run multiple prompts in a queue and auto-download the results.

Does KoseFlow work without a Google account?

No. You need to sign in with a Google account that has access to Google Flow (labs.google). KoseFlow uses your Google session to communicate with Flow on your behalf.

What is the daily limit on the free plan?

Free accounts can run up to 30 prompts per day. The counter resets every 24 hours. Pro plan users have no daily limit.

How do I activate my Pro license?

After purchasing, you'll receive a license key by email. Open the KoseFlow side panel, click "Get Pro", then click "Already have a license key?" and enter your key.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you're not satisfied within 7 days of purchase, contact us at ai.kose.lab@gmail.com and we'll process a full refund.

How do I request deletion of my data?

Send a data deletion request to ai.kose.lab@gmail.com. We will permanently delete your account data within 7 days.

KoseFlow is not working — what should I do?

Make sure you're on a Google Flow project page (labs.google). If the issue persists, try reloading the extension from chrome://extensions and contact us with a description of the problem.
